Technical Guidelines & FAQ

Explore technical insights and compliance frameworks governing the manufacture and transport of Optisol products.

How does Neutrasafe differ from general acid-based rust removers?
Neutrasafe is formulated with a completely neutral pH of 5.5 to 6.0, matching the natural pH of human skin. Unlike harsh phosphoric or hydrochloric acid removers (which strip base metal and cause dangerous fumes and surface pitting), Neutrasafe selectively targets rust and leaves base metal unaffected.
What dimensions and variations are available for VCI Plastic and Paper?
To accommodate various component scales, both VCI Plastic and VCI Paper are supplied as continuous rolls, customized pre-cut sheets, and flat 2D or robust 3D block-bottom bags. This ensures swift manual or automated packing lines for machinery export cycles.
Can the Support Angles withstand high-humidity marine conditions?
Absolutely. Our Support Angles (edge protection boards) are crafted from highly compressed, water-resistant fibers and combined with external VCI wraps. They offer crucial structural reinforcement and shock protection without absorbing humidity inside shipping containers.
