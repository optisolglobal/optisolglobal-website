How It Works

How Optisol Rust Remover works

A pH-neutral chemistry that targets rust — and only rust.

01

Apply Directly

Pour or spray straight onto the corroded surface — no dilution, no pre-treatment.

02

Rust Dissolves

The pH-neutral formula breaks down iron oxide on contact, lifting rust away from the base metal.

03

Metal Stays Safe

Unlike acid-based removers, it will not pit, etch, or weaken the underlying metal surface.

04

Ready to Finish

Rinse and the surface is clean, bare, and ready for painting, coating, or immediate use.
